# greenify-system
Digital platform for plants, customization, and smart care solutions.

---

## 📌 Overview

Greenify is a modern digital platform that brings together plant purchasing, customized greenery design, and smart plant care assistance into one seamless experience.

The platform aims to simplify how users explore, select, and manage plants while enhancing convenience, personalization, and decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Provide a centralized marketplace for plants and gardening products  
- Enable users to customize plant arrangements and bouquets  
- Offer intelligent plant care guidance  
- Deliver a smooth and user-friendly digital experience  

---

## 🚀 Features

### 🌿 Plant Marketplace
- Browse and purchase plants and gardening tools  
- Filter and search products easily  
- Secure and simple checkout process  

### 🎨 Custom Design
- Create personalized plant arrangements  
- Customize bouquets based on user preferences  
- Upload reference images for inspiration  

### 🤖 Smart Assistance
- AI-based plant care recommendations  
- Guidance for plant selection and maintenance  

### ⚡ Performance & UX
- Fast and responsive interface  
- Clean and intuitive design  
- Seamless navigation
